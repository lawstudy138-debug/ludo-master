How to Play

🎯 Objective

Move all 4 of your tokens from start to home. First to finish wins.

🎲 Dice

Roll a 6 to bring a token out. Extra turn on 6. Three 6s in a row → turn skipped.

✂️ Capture

Land on an opponent’s token to send it back to start (except safe zones).

🏠 Safe Zones

Starred cells and your own color path are safe.

🏁 Home

Exact number required to enter home. All 4 tokens home = finish.
